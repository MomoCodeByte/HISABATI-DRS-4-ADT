Ukurasa wa 113

Mfano wa kwanza. Andika sehemu nyingine nne zilizosawa na 1/2. Njia: 1/2 = 1/2 × 2/2 = 2/4. 1/2 = 1/2 × 3/3 = 3/6. 1/2 = 1/2 × 4/4 = 4/8. 1/2 = 1/2 × 5/5 = 5/10. Kwa hiyo, sehemu nyingine nne zilizosawa na 1/2 ni: 2/4 = 3/6 = 4/8 = 5/10. Mfano wa pili. Andika sehemu nyingine nne zilizosawa na 3/4. Njia: 3/4 = 3/4 × 2/2 = 6/8. 3/4 = 3/4 × 3/3 = 9/12. 3/4 = 3/4 × 4/4 = 12/16. 3/4 = 3/4 × 5/5 = 15/20. Kwa hiyo, sehemu nyingine nne zilizosawa na 3/4 ni: 6/8 = 9/12 = 12/16 = 15/20.
